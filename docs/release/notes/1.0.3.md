# Pasto 1.0.3

A better first run.

- **A new welcome**: Pasto now sets everything up before you start — pasting into other apps, clipboard access and smart suggestions each have their own step, with their state shown live. Every step has its own illustration, and moving between steps is instant.
- **Screen recording without the dead end**: when macOS quits Pasto after you allow screen recording, Pasto comes back by itself, where you left it, and no longer asks again for a permission you already gave.
- **Never an empty panel**: after the welcome, your history starts with three short notes on how to paste, search and pin, and a pinboard with links to Pasto's site, help and release notes. Delete them whenever you like; they do not come back.
- **Tips in the panel**: the first time you open Pasto, five short labels point out search, pinboards, suggestions and the shortcuts on each card. *Help › Show Tips* shows them again.
- **A proper installer window**: the disk image now opens with its background, the app and the Applications folder in place.
- **Safer launches**: start-up alerts always come to the front, two copies of Pasto can no longer be open at once, and every launch leaves a small record that helps diagnose a problem.
